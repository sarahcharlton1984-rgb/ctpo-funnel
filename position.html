Where the numbers come from. 2026/27 rates and thresholds as published by HMRC: corporation tax 19% to 25% with marginal relief, dividend tax 10.75% / 35.75% / 39.35%, employee NI 8% then 2%, employer NI 15% above a £5,000 threshold, CGT 18% / 24% with Business Asset Disposal Relief at 18% on the first £1m, IHT 40% above a £325,000 nil-rate band and a £175,000 residence nil-rate band that tapers away above a £2m estate. Combined agricultural and business property relief is capped at £2.5m per person from 6 April 2026, transferable between spouses. Unused pensions count in the estate from 6 April 2027, as legislated in Finance Act 2026.

What this is not. An estimate on the figures you typed, not advice on your affairs, and it can only see what you have entered. It does not know your share classes, your loan account history, your spouse's estate or your shareholders' agreement. Business Property Relief in particular is a rounded judgement on full evidence, not something any tool can settle. Sarah Charlton is a tax strategist and the founder of Tax 4 Pros. Rates change at the Budget on 28 October 2026.
