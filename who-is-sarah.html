Why I do this work.

I am Sarah Charlton, tax strategist and founder of Tax 4 Pros. I spent years watching UK company directors do everything right on the operations of their business and then hand over half of the outcome to the state at the wrong moments: on extraction, on sale, and on the estate that gets left behind. There was plenty they could have done about it. Nobody had ever sat them down and shown them the structure that was available to them, legally and in the open.

Tax 4 Pros is the accountancy and compliance arm. It runs the numbers and keeps you properly compliant. CT Private Office is the strategy layer on top: it designs the structure, then builds it. Family Investment Companies, holding structures, growth shares, incorporations, business exits, inheritance and residence. Every piece defensible, sourced, and signed off. Never a scheme, never a product sale.

Two firms because the work is genuinely two different disciplines. One point of contact because the alternative is you doing the coordinating.

Work we have done

Three recent CT Private Office cases, anonymised.

Real engagements. Details and numbers have been altered to prevent identification; the shape of each plan is real.

Case one · Family business, exit & relocation

Founders of a growing UK trading company

Live plan to place an offshore holding company over the trading company via a share for share exchange, with HMRC clearance, rolling over a seven figure deferred gain. Growth shares layered in for the next generation, at nominal value above a valuation hurdle, so value above that hurdle builds outside the founders' estate without disturbing the exchange or the founders' control. A residence plan sequenced alongside.

Deferred gain protected: seven figures · future estate growth outside 40% IHT
Case two · IHT & estate

Retiring director selling company, worldwide estate

Estate in the mid seven figures across UK property, investments, pension and an overseas trading business. Long term UK resident, so worldwide estate in scope under the 2025 rules. Costed plan covering spouse exemption planning, Business Property Relief on the overseas business, gifts from surplus income, and a Family Investment Company for the buy out proceeds, coordinated with the client's solicitor for the will.

Headline IHT exposure identified: seven figures · costed plan to bring it down legitimately
Case three · Growth freeze

Serial entrepreneur, new venture & property portfolio

Personal estate already sitting on the £2m taper. A new business worth almost nothing today, projected to grow hard. Growth shares to his adult children at nominal value, above a valuation hurdle, so value above that hurdle builds outside his estate. Control retained via freezer shares. Property portfolio incorporation modelled honestly, both routes fully costed.

Future growth modelled outside the estate: at 40%, that is £400,000 on the first £1m of growth, before reliefs
